04 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

BEFORE YOU
REACH OUT.

A few things people usually want to know before starting a project with us.

How long does a typical project take?

Most websites launch in 4–8 weeks from kickoff, depending on scope and how quickly we get content and feedback from you. Marketing and social work runs as an ongoing monthly retainer once the build is live.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short brief, a few examples of sites or brands you like, and any existing content — logo, copy, product photos. If it isn't ready yet, we help pull it together during the Discover stage.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

Two structured revision rounds are included in the Build stage. Additional rounds can be added if the scope changes significantly after strategy sign-off.

Do you support the site after it launches?

Yes. Every launch includes a QA pass across devices and a walkthrough of how to make updates. From there you can choose ongoing SEO, ads or social support, or manage things yourself.

Can we hire you for just marketing or social, without a new website?

Yes. Websites, social and marketing are run as separate crafts internally, so you can start with whichever one your business needs most right now.
